My dearest Riddhi,

I wanted to make you something that you could open and feel a little warmth, even on a day when I am not right beside you. I don't think one letter could ever explain what you mean to me. You are not just someone I talk to; you are someone whose presence makes the ordinary feel a little more special. Even the smallest moments can become precious when they have you in them. I hope you never doubt how deserving you are of love, patience, respect, laughter, and every soft beautiful thing this world can offer. On days when you feel tired, I hope you remember that you do not have to be perfect to be wonderful. You already are enough, exactly as you are. I wish I could wrap every good thought I have about you into a thousand tiny stars and leave them around you, so wherever you look, you would find a reminder that you are cherished. Keep that beautiful heart safe. Keep dreaming. Keep being wonderfully you. And whenever you need a little reminder: somewhere, someone is smiling just because you exist. Happy Princess Day, my favourite girl. ♡
With all my love, always ♡
